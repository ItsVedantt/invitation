To Our Respected Professors & Mentors

Presented with reverence by The Neuronauts Student Body

Dear Esteemed Professors,

Behind every scientific breakthrough, every line of innovative code, and every soaring ambition lies the patient guidance of a teacher. You have not merely taught us the principles of technology, neuroscience, and logic; you have sculpted our character, nurtured our curiosity, and ignited within us the courage to question and create.

On this auspicious occasion of Teacher's Day 2026, the students of the Neuronauts Forum humbly request the honor of your gracious presence at our special tribute celebration.

We have curated a memorable day of heartfelt felicitations, lighthearted camaraderie, cultural performances, interactive laughter, and tokens of our unending gratitude. Please join us as we turn the spotlight onto those who illuminate our world every single day.

With Profound Respect & Admiration,

The Neuronauts Forum

Student Organizing Committee, Batch 2026
